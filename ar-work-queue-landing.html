Everything your team needs to
work AR without spreadsheets

Import, Don't Retype

2

A Queue for Every Biller

How It Works

Three steps, no spreadsheets

1

Upload your aging report

Import the report your team already runs. Claims are grouped by payor and facility, and balances refresh with every new import.

2

Assign and work the queue

Your team logs in, works their claims, adds notes and updates statuses. Pending statuses carry a follow-up date so nothing is forgotten.

3

See what is working

Managers track who is working what, which claims have gone stale, and which denial reasons keep costing you revenue.
